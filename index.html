01Why we built this

Recruiting was broken.

Logan Raven was a varsity basketball captain and dual-sport athlete in Marin County, California, and he still couldn't figure out where to post his highlights or how to reach coaches. The tools were broken in two ways.

$700/mo

The good tools cost a fortune

Built for families with money, not the kid grinding alone in the backyard. If you couldn't pay, you didn't get seen.

∞noise

The free tools were a black hole

Completely overcrowded with no real discovery algorithm. You were a needle in a haystack, invisible to the coaches who mattered.

No single hub. No equity. No platform built for the athlete first.

04How it works

Three steps to get seen.

No agents. No paywall. No gatekeepers. Just you, your game, and the coaches who should be watching.

01

Build your profile

Import your Hudl highlights, add your grades and test scores, list your positions and stats, and link every social. Takes about five minutes.

02

Get discovered

Our algorithm surfaces you to coaches by position, level, region, and academics, not by who paid the most. Talent does the talking.

03

Connect with coaches

Coaches find you, save your profile, and watch your film. For the first time, recruiting runs in your favor.
